Terms of Service & Code of Ethics

This agreement governs the use of Noxway by Noctiva. It explicitly embodies the professional and ethical standards prescribed by the ACM/IEEE Software Engineering Code of Ethics and the Colegio de Ingenieros del Perú (CIP).

1. Public Interest & Human Well-Being (ACM/IEEE Principle 1 / CIP Art. 1)

Noctiva commits to engineering software that preserves the physical integrity, dignity, and peace of mind of night-shift workers and their families. All algorithmic decisions prioritize user safety above commercial gain or engagement metrics.

2. Geolocation Privacy & Restricted Data Collection (ACM/IEEE Principle 2 & 3)

User telemetry is collected strictly on an opt-in basis and exclusively while a commute check-in is active. Once safe arrival is confirmed, tracking terminates immediately. Coordinate logs are encrypted, never sold, and never shared with third-party advertisers.

3. Companion View & Consent Protocols

Trusted contacts are invited exclusively by the worker. The companion view provides status visibility without invasive surveillance. Both parties may revoke permissions at any moment without penalty.

4. Non-Replacement of Emergency Services (ACM/IEEE Principle 4)

Noxway is a preventative and mutual-aid tool. It does not replace official national emergency response authorities (PNP 105, Bomberos 116, SAMU 106). In life-threatening emergencies, users are urged to contact emergency dispatch directly.

5. Open-Source Transparency & Academic Integrity (CIP)

This project complies with open-source licensing principles, rigorous documentation standards, and ethical peer review in accordance with international software engineering guidelines.
